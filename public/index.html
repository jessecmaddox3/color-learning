How it works

You start with 11 familiar colors and practice five new ones at a time. Three correct answers in a row master a color. A mistake resets that color’s streak.

The full 949-name xkcd survey list is included, with some informal and profane names. Color appearance depends on your screen and vision. This is a vocabulary game, not a vision test.

No AI subscription, account or internet connection is needed to play a downloaded copy.

Learners and saving

Keep a copy

Exports contain this learner’s nickname and progress. Store them somewhere private. Import creates a separate local learner.

Clearing browser data can erase device saves. Private browsing may not keep them after closing. A recovery export keeps alternate snapshots after conflicting saves.

Start fresh

Reset keeps this learner and starts their progress over. If connected, the reset also saves to cloud. Removing a local learner keeps any cloud copy.
